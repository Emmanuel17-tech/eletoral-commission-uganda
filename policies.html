Accessibility, Privacy & Terms

How this website concept is intended to be used and improved.

On this page Accessibility Privacy Terms of use
Access for everyone

Accessibility statement

This is a website design concept and has not been formally audited. The design aims to support clear, inclusive access, including responsive layouts, readable content, keyboard-operable controls, and reduced-motion preferences where applicable.

Help us improve

If you encounter a barrier or need information in another format, please use the contact page. Before any public launch, the site should be tested with assistive technologies and reviewed against the applicable accessibility requirements.

Your information

Privacy notice

This demonstration site is a front-end concept. It does not currently send or store contact form submissions, and no analytics or account features are configured. The contact form only displays a local confirmation message in your browser.

Before a live launch

If this concept is connected to a production service, the responsible organization should publish an approved privacy notice explaining what personal information is collected, why it is used, how it is protected, how long it is retained, and how people can exercise their rights. Do not submit sensitive personal information through this demo.

Using this concept

Terms of use

This website is a design concept and is not an official Electoral Commission website. Text, notices, images, contact details, and service descriptions may be placeholders and should not be treated as official guidance.

Important information

  • Confirm election, registration, and results information through verified official channels.
  • Do not rely on this concept for legal, electoral, or time-sensitive decisions.
  • Content and availability may change as the concept is developed.
  • Before publication, the site owner should approve and replace all sample content and confirm rights to use all materials.

These statements are draft website-concept content, not approved legal policies. They should be reviewed by the responsible organization before publication.
